Rank distribution
Share of all players at each rank or better ("top X%"). Used by Rank distribution in the Ranks editor. Beginner scenarios use ranks 1-4 (Wool to Stellar), Intermediate 5-8, Expert 9-12.
Scale for each tier's audience
Intermediate and Expert scenarios are mostly played by people who already cleared the tier below, so top 30% of everyone is a bigger slice of that scenario's own leaderboard. Audience = share of all players who play that tier (default: everyone who reached the top rank of the tier below).
